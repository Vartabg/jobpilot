Why might a form ask?

Field help or an employer’s stated purpose appears with each finding when available. If the inspected field does not explain a purpose, the report says so. General reporting practices do not establish what a particular company does with your answers.

  • Some race data collection supports applicant-flow monitoring. The EEOC advises keeping it separate from qualification and selection information. EEOC guidance on race inquiries.
  • Questions about sex, sexual orientation, or transgender status can raise concerns about relevance and discrimination. A flag asks you to review the request; it is not a legal finding. EEOC guidance on gender inquiries.
  • Covered federal contractors invite veteran self-identification for compliance and outreach. Applicant disclosure is voluntary. DOL veteran self-identification FAQs.
