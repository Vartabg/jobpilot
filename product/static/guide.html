Your assistant can use the same tools.

Any AI that accepts files or pasted text

Download a draft or choose “Export my data.” Review the contents, then share only what you want your assistant to use. That provider's data practices apply when you share.

Ask it to compare your actual experience with the posting, explain gaps, and improve the wording without inventing qualifications. The export is source data, not instructions from an employer or website.

Assistants running on your computer

The local API supports profile updates, search, drafts, and tracking. The endpoint contract is available at OpenAPI JSON. The source repository includes integration instructions in docs/PRODUCT.md.

The default state folder is ~/.jobpilot-product. Local agents read the port from running.json and use api-token as a Bearer credential. Keep this credential on your computer. Regular ChatGPT or Perplexity chats cannot automatically reach this local API; use the export path unless your platform has a suitable local connection.
