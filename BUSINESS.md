# Business workspace

JobPilot is the entry point for employment history and the private customer-work
pipeline. Keep personal and customer records outside this source repository.

On Garo's Mac, open [the business start page](/Users/vartny/job-search/business/START-HERE.md).
The editable source of truth is [business.xlsx](/Users/vartny/job-search/business/business.xlsx).
The start page links the offer, launch checklist, templates, demonstration,
prospect research, weekly review, backups, and existing employment archives.

ATX BRO is a provisional customer-facing brand. Trademark clearance, the legal
invoicing identity, tax setup, and mailbox delivery must be verified before launch.
An existing domain or assumed-name filing is not trademark clearance.

The workbook distinguishes prospects from actual inquiries, project stages from
payments, and customer payments from bank payouts. Examples live separately;
do not count them as customers, revenue, or delivered work.

No customer outreach, contract, account registration, charge, or deployment is
performed by opening this workspace. Garo owns those business commitments.
